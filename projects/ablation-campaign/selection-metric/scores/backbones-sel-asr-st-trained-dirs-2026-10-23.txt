Selection score with speech translation, IFT-stage backbone arms — ST restricted
to the trained directions (checkpoint-6310, whisper-large-v3 encoder + MoE
adapter, 700 h/lang).

Supersedes the ST definition of backbones-sel-asr-st-2026-10-23.txt, which is
kept as the record of what was defined before the PI's 2026-10-23 instruction.
The ASR part is the metric of README.md unchanged (frozen 2026-09-23) and is
identical in both files; only the ST half changed.

  ASR   sel = (1.0*med(ID) + 1.5*med(OOD-train) + 0.6*med(OOD-related)) / 3.1
        medians of clipped per-language CER; ID en,de,es,fr,it in-domain;
        OOD-train the same five on FLEURS; OOD-related pt,ro,nl,da,sv.

  ST    s_l = 1 - chrF_l/100, lowercase plain chrF, 100 clips per direction,
        over the five directions the IFT mix trains:
          de,es,fr,it -> en   (YODAS Granary `ast`, 4.7-10.5% of group draws)
          en -> de            (CoVoST2, 429.9 h, 9.6% of group draws)
        sel(ST) = 1 - med(chrF over the five)/100
        The zero-shot related group (pt,ro,nl,da,sv -> en) that the 2026-10-23
        first draft weighted at 0.6 is NOT in this score: the PI asked for ST
        on the languages and directions the arms were trained on.

  headline  sel(ASR+ST) = (sel(ASR) + sel(ST)) / 2                     lower better
